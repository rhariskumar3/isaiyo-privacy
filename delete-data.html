Isaiyo

Offline music player for Android

Delete Your Data

Isaiyo stores everything locally on your device. You can delete it at any time, and your music files are never touched.

✅ Isaiyo never uploads your data to any server. Deletion happens instantly and permanently on your device — no request to us is needed.

Method 1 — Delete everything (uninstall the app)

1
Open your Android SettingsGo to Settings → Apps → Isaiyo
2
Tap "Uninstall"The library index, playlists, favourites, listening history, queue and settings are permanently deleted
3
DoneYour songs stay exactly where they were — Isaiyo only read them. Nothing remains on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Method 2 — Clear storage (keep the app installed)

1
Open your Android SettingsGo to Settings → Apps → Isaiyo → Storage & cache
2
Tap "Clear storage"Removes the index, playlists, favourites, history, queue and settings; the app returns to its first-launch screen
3
Optional: "Clear cache"Removes cached artwork thumbnails and any temporary audio from Isaiyo Together

Method 3 — Delete individual items in the app

ItemWhereEffect
A playlistPlaylists → the playlist → ⋮ → DeleteRemoved immediately; songs stay in your library
A favouriteTap the heart in Now Playing or the song's menuRemoved from Favourites
The queueNow Playing → Up next → ClearQueue and resume position cleared
Recent searchesSearch → ✕ next to a recent searchThat search is forgotten
Library indexSettings → Library → RescanRebuilt from the files currently on the device; removed files disappear

What about my music files?

Isaiyo never modifies, moves or deletes your audio files. Deleting data in Isaiyo removes only Isaiyo's own records. To remove a song from your phone, delete the file with a file manager or the app you used to add it.
