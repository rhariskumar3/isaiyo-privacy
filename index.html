📋 Overview

Isaiyo ("the app") is an offline music player for Android that plays audio files already stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server of ours anywhere in the picture.

This policy explains what the app stores, which permissions it asks for and why, and how you delete your data.

💾 What the App Stores (On-Device Only)

Isaiyo keeps the following information only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • An index of the audio files on your device (title, artist, album, duration, file location) read from Android's media library
  • Playlists and favourites you create
  • Your listening history and the current queue, so playback can resume where you left off
  • Recent searches
  • Your settings (theme, language, scan and playback preferences)
Isaiyo does not collect, transmit or store any data on external servers. The app does not request internet access. The only way anything leaves your phone is Isaiyo Together, a local connection you start yourself.

📡 Isaiyo Together (optional, Android 12 and newer)

Isaiyo Together lets phones in the same room play the same music at the same time. One phone hosts; others join. It uses Google Play services' Nearby Connections, which links the phones directly over Bluetooth and local Wi‑Fi — not over the internet.

  • Nothing happens until you open the Together screen and tap Start or Join. Both sides confirm with a code shown on each phone.
  • While a group is running, the host phone sends the audio of the current song, its title, artist and position, and your phone's name (as set in Android) to the phones in the group. Nothing is stored by the host about the listeners, and nothing is sent to us or to any server.
  • Receivers keep a temporary copy of the audio in the app's cache only for as long as it plays; it is removed when the song changes or the group ends.
  • Leaving the group, or ending it as host, disconnects immediately.

🚗 Android Auto, widget and lock screen

When connected to Android Auto, the car display shows your library and playback controls. The home-screen widget, the media notification and the lock screen show the song that is playing. All of them read the same on-device data; Isaiyo sends nothing to Google or the car manufacturer beyond what Android needs to draw those screens.

🗑️ Data Deletion

All app data lives in your device's private storage. You have full control:

  • Uninstall the app — permanently and immediately deletes the index, playlists, favourites, history and settings
  • Clear storage — Android Settings → Apps → Isaiyo → Storage → Clear storage, keeping the app installed
  • In the app — delete a playlist from its menu, remove favourites with the heart, clear the queue from Now Playing

Your audio files themselves are never modified or deleted by Isaiyo. For step-by-step instructions see the Data Deletion page.

🧩 Third Parties

Isaiyo does not use any third-party SDK that collects user data. There are:

  • No advertisements
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No crash-reporting services
  • No social login and no sharing SDKs

Isaiyo Together uses Google Play services' Nearby Connections on your phone to link devices directly; Google's own privacy policy governs Play services. Isaiyo sends it nothing beyond what is needed to connect the phones in the room.

👶 Children's Privacy

Isaiyo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
